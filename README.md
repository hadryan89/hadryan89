## About

I'm Hadryan Fortinis, an Intelligent Systems student at FATEC Pompeia. I work between full stack development and artificial intelligence: I build web applications with Angular, TypeScript and Node.js, and study neural networks, computer vision and language models. I'm open to collaborations and open source projects.

## Research

I'm a **FAPESP undergraduate research fellow**, researching artificial intelligence and cloud computing applied to agribusiness. The work covers:

- Computer vision on drone, satellite and field imagery to analyze crops
- Precision agriculture models that detect patterns, anomalies and crop health indicators
- Cloud pipelines on AWS, GCP and Azure for training and deploying models
- Collecting and organizing agricultural datasets
- LLMs as an interpretation and decision-support layer in the field

## Currently studying

**Fine-tuning LLMs with LoRA and QLoRA.** Adapting pre-trained language and vision-language models to a specific domain by training small low-rank adapters instead of the full model, which makes it possible to specialize large models on a single GPU. My focus is on preparing instruction datasets, choosing adapter hyperparameters and evaluating the fine-tuned model against the base one.
